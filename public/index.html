Our approach

Writing is treated as a form of thinking.

We do not begin with templates. Students are taught to identify the real question inside a prompt, distinguish assertion from argument, recognize hidden assumptions, and build a line of reasoning that is both original and defensible.

The result is stronger writing, but also stronger intellectual habits: better reading, better judgment, and greater confidence when approaching unfamiliar subjects.

The process

A serious essay is built in stages.

1
Question

Define the problem precisely and identify what is actually at stake.

2
Position

Develop a thesis with enough tension and specificity to matter.

3
Reasoning

Build the chain of claims, evidence, distinctions, and objections.

4
Revision

Refine structure, prose, and intellectual precision until the argument is clean.

Mentors
